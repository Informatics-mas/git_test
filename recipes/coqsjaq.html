coquilles Saint Jacques Recipe

coquilles Saint Jacques is a delicate and elegant seafood dish that highlights the natural sweetness of scallops. For 2026, chefs are focusing on using fresh, high-quality scallops and simple preparations to let the seafood shine.

Ingredients (Serves 4)

  • 12 to 16 large sea scallops (fresh is best)
  • 7 oz (200g) button mushrooms, thinly sliced/li>
  • 2 shallots, finely minced
  • 2/3 cup (150ml) dry white wine (like Chardonnay or Sauvignon Blanc)
  • 3/4 cup (200ml) heavy cream (crème fraîche)
  • 3 tbsp (40g) unsalted butter
  • 1 egg yolk (to thicken and enrich the sauce)
  • 1/2 cup grated Gruyère or Comté cheese
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs (for extra crunch)
  • Salt, black pepper, and fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. 1-Prep the Scallops: Pat the scallops very dry with paper towels. In a skillet over medium-high heat, melt a knob of butter. Sear the scallops for about 1 minute per side until just golden but still translucent inside. Remove and set aside.
  2. 2-Sauté the Aromatics: In the same pan, add another bit of butter and sauté the minced shallots and sliced mushrooms until the liquid has evaporated and they are slightly browned.
  3. 3-Make the Sauce: Pour in the white wine to deglaze the pan, scraping up the browned bits. Let it simmer until reduced by half. Stir in the heavy cream and let it thicken slightly for 2 minutes.
  4. 4-Liaison: Remove the pan from the heat. Whisk the egg yolk in a small bowl with a spoonful of the warm sauce, then pour the mixture back into the pan. Stir constantly to create a velvety texture (do not boil again or the egg will scramble).
  5. 5-Assembly: Place the scallops back into the sauce or divide the mixture into 4 individual oven-safe scallop shells or ramekins.
  6. 6-Broil: Sprinkle with the grated cheese and breadcrumbs. Top with a tiny dot of butter. Place under the oven broiler (grill) for 3 to 5 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  7. 7-Serve: Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.
