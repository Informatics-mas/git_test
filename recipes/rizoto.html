Rizoto Recipe

Rizoto, or risotto, is a creamy Italian rice dish that requires patience and attention to detail. For 2026, chefs are emphasizing the use of high-quality Arborio rice and fresh ingredients to enhance the flavor and texture of the dish.

Ingredients (Serves 4)

  • 1.5 cups (300g) Arborio or Carnaroli rice (high-starch risotto rice)
  • 5 cups (1.2L) chicken or vegetable broth, kept warm
  • 2 large leeks, white and light green parts only, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup (120ml) dry white wine
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese (Parmigiano-Reggiano)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • Optional: A squeeze of lemon juice for brightness

Instructions

  1. 1-Sauté the Leeks: In a large, heavy-bottomed pan or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon of butter over medium heat. Add the sliced leeks and a pinch of salt. Cook slowly for 5–7 minutes until they are soft and translucent, but not browned.
  2. 2-Toast the Rice: Add the rice to the pan with the leeks. Stir for 2 minutes until the edges of the rice grains become translucent and they are well-coated in oil/butter. This "toasting" step is crucial for flavor and texture.
  3. 3-Deglaze: Pour in the white wine. Stir constantly until the wine has been completely absorbed by the rice.
  4. 4-The "Slow Pour" Process: Add the warm broth one ladle at a time (about 1/2 cup). Stir frequently. Wait until the liquid is almost fully absorbed before adding the next ladle. This stirring action releases the starch, creating the signature creamy sauce.
  5. 5-Cook to Al Dente: Continue adding broth for about 18–22 minutes. The rice should be tender but still have a slight "bite" (al dente) in the center. You may not need all the broth.
  6. 6-The "Mantecatura" (Finishing): Remove the pan from the heat. This is the secret to a perfect risotto. Vigorously stir in the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter and the grated Parmesan.
  7. 7-Rest and Serve: Cover the pan and let it sit for 2 minutes. If the risotto looks too thick, add a tiny splash of warm broth to loosen it. Serve immediately on warm plates.
