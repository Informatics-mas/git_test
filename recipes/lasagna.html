Lasagna Recipe

Making a classic lasagna involves three main components: a rich meat sauce, a creamy cheese filling, and layered pasta. For the best results in 2026, many chefs recommend a mix of ground beef and Italian sausage for depth of flavor.

Ingredients

  • Meat Sauce:
  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 1 diced onion
  • 3 cloves minced garlic
  • 28 oz crushed tomatoes
  • 15 oz tomato sauce
  • 6 oz tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • Cheese Filling:
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg (beaten)
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Layers:
  • 12 lasagna noodles (standard or oven-ready)
  • 1 lb shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. 1-Prepare the Sauce: Brown the beef and sausage with onion and garlic in a large pot. Drain excess fat. Stir in all tomato products, sugar, and seasonings. Simmer on low for at least 30–45 minutes to let flavors meld
  2. 2-Boil the Noodles: If using standard noodles, boil them in salted water until al dente (usually 8–10 minutes). Drain and set aside on parchment paper to prevent sticking.
  3. 3-Mix Cheeses: In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta, egg, parsley, and salt. Some recipes also add a portion of the Parmesan here for extra sharpness.
      4. 4-Assemble the Layers
    1. 1-Bottom: Spread 1–2 cups of meat sauce to prevent sticking.
    2. 2-Layers: Place 3–4 noodles, spread 1/3 of the ricotta mixture, and sprinkle with mozzarella and Parmesan. Repeat twice more.
    3. 3-Top: Finish with a final layer of noodles topped with the remaining sauce and a generous amount of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  4. 5-Bake: Cover tightly with foil (spray the foil with cooking spray so it doesn't stick to the cheese). Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for another 20–25 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  5. 6-Rest: Allow the lasagna to rest for 15–20 minutes before slicing. This is crucial for the layers to set so the lasagna holds its shape when served.
